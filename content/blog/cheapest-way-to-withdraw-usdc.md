USDC (USD Coin) is the second most popular stablecoin after USDT. As a regulated and transparent stablecoin, many traders prefer USDC for its reliability. But withdrawal fees vary widely across exchanges and networks.

## USDC Withdrawal Fee Comparison

### Ethereum (ERC-20)
- Binance: $3.00–$5.00
- Coinbase: $1.00–$3.00
- Kraken: $2.50
- OKX: $3.00–$5.00

### Solana (SPL)
- Binance: $1.00
- Coinbase: $0.01–$0.50
- OKX: $0.80

### Arbitrum (L2)
- Binance: $0.50
- Coinbase: $0.10
- OKX: $0.30

### Base (L2)
- Coinbase: $0.01–$0.10 (native network)

### Polygon
- Binance: $0.10
- OKX: $0.10

## The Cheapest Options

**Coinbase on Base:** If both sides support Base L2, this is essentially free ($0.01–$0.10).

**Polygon:** Widely supported and costs under $0.10 on most exchanges.

**Solana SPL:** $0.50–$1.00 with near-instant confirmation.

## USDC vs USDT: Which Is Cheaper to Transfer?

In most cases, USDC and USDT have similar withdrawal fees on the same networks. The key differences:

- **USDC on Base** is uniquely cheap due to Coinbase's integration
- **USDT on TRC-20** is available on more exchanges than USDC on TRC-20
- Both are approximately equal on Solana and Polygon

## When to Use USDC Over USDT

- When transferring via Coinbase (Base L2 is cheapest)
- When regulatory compliance matters (USDC is fully audited)
- When interacting with DeFi protocols on Arbitrum or Optimism (USDC often has deeper liquidity)
