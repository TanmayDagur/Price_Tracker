One of the easiest ways to reduce your crypto trading costs is to enable BNB fee deduction on Binance. This simple setting gives you an instant 25% discount on every trade. Here is exactly how to set it up and how much you will save.

## How Much Do You Save?

Without BNB discount:
- Maker fee: 0.10%
- Taker fee: 0.10%

With BNB discount:
- Maker fee: **0.075%**
- Taker fee: **0.075%**

On a $10,000 trade, that is:
- Without BNB: $10 fee
- With BNB: $7.50 fee
- **Savings per trade: $2.50**

If you make 10 trades per day: **$25/day = $750/month saved.**

## How to Enable BNB Fee Deduction

1. Log in to your Binance account
2. Click on your profile icon → **Dashboard**
3. Scroll down to **Using BNB to Pay for Fees**
4. Toggle the switch to **ON**
5. Ensure you have some BNB in your spot wallet

That is it. Binance will automatically deduct fees in BNB at the discounted rate.

## How Much BNB Do You Need?

You need enough BNB to cover your trading fees. A rough guideline:

- Trading $1,000/day: Keep ~$1 of BNB
- Trading $10,000/day: Keep ~$10 of BNB
- Trading $100,000/day: Keep ~$100 of BNB

We recommend keeping at least **$50 of BNB** in your account to avoid running out during active trading sessions.

## Does It Stack With VIP Discounts?

Yes! The BNB discount applies on top of your VIP tier discount. If you are a VIP 1 trader with 0.09% taker fee, the BNB discount brings it down to **0.0675%**.
