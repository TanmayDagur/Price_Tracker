Binance and Kraken are two of the most popular exchanges for serious crypto traders. But when it comes to arbitrage, the fee structures can make or break your strategy. Let us break down every cost you will encounter on both platforms.

## Trading Fee Comparison

### Binance
- **Maker fee:** 0.1% (0.075% with BNB discount)
- **Taker fee:** 0.1% (0.075% with BNB discount)
- **VIP tiers:** Volume-based discounts down to 0.02% maker / 0.04% taker

### Kraken
- **Maker fee:** 0.16%
- **Taker fee:** 0.26%
- **Pro tiers:** Volume-based discounts down to 0.00% maker / 0.10% taker

**Winner: Binance.** At the base tier, Binance is significantly cheaper. A round trip (buy + sell) costs 0.2% on Binance vs 0.42% on Kraken. That is more than double.

## Withdrawal Fee Comparison (Top 10 Coins)

- **BTC:** Binance 0.00001 BTC ($0.67) vs Kraken 0.00015 BTC ($10.12)
- **ETH:** Binance 0.00028 ETH ($0.98) vs Kraken 0.0015 ETH ($5.25)
- **USDT (TRC-20):** Binance $1.00 vs Kraken $2.50
- **SOL:** Binance 0.01 SOL ($1.50) vs Kraken 0.015 SOL ($2.25)
- **XRP:** Binance 0.25 XRP ($0.15) vs Kraken 0.02 XRP ($0.01)

**Winner: Mostly Binance**, except for XRP where Kraken is significantly cheaper.

## Deposit Fees

Both exchanges offer **free deposits** for most cryptocurrencies. Fiat deposits vary:
- Binance: Free for bank transfer, 1.8% for credit card
- Kraken: Free for bank transfer, no credit card option in most regions

## Which Is Better for Arbitrage?

For pure arbitrage trading, **Binance is the clear winner** due to lower trading and withdrawal fees. However, Kraken often has **slightly different prices** than Binance, making it an excellent target for cross-exchange arbitrage.

The optimal strategy is to:
1. Hold funds on **both** exchanges
2. Monitor spreads using our [Binance vs Kraken comparison](/compare/binance-vs-kraken)
3. Execute trades when net profit is positive after all fees
4. Periodically rebalance funds using the cheapest withdrawal network
