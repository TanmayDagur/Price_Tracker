## Using SOL as a Transfer Medium

Even if you do not trade Solana, you can use it as a cheap way to move value between exchanges:

1. Convert your asset (e.g., USDT) to SOL on Exchange A
2. Withdraw SOL to Exchange B (fee: ~$1.00–$1.50)
3. Convert SOL back to your desired asset on Exchange B

This is often cheaper than transferring USDT directly, especially if only ERC-20 is available for USDT on one of the exchanges.
