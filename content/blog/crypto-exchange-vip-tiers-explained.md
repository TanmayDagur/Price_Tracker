Every major crypto exchange offers tiered fee structures where higher trading volume unlocks lower fees. Understanding these tiers can save you thousands of dollars over time.

## How VIP Tiers Work

Exchanges track your **30-day trading volume** and automatically assign you a tier. Higher tiers mean lower maker and taker fees. Most exchanges have 8–10 tiers.

## Binance VIP Tiers

| Tier | 30d Volume | Maker Fee | Taker Fee |
|------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Regular | < $1M | 0.10% | 0.10% |
| VIP 1 | $1M+ | 0.09% | 0.10% |
| VIP 2 | $5M+ | 0.08% | 0.10% |
| VIP 3 | $10M+ | 0.07% | 0.08% |
| VIP 4 | $25M+ | 0.06% | 0.07% |
| VIP 5 | $50M+ | 0.05% | 0.06% |

With BNB discount applied, these rates drop by an additional 25%.

## Impact on Arbitrage Profitability

The difference between base tier and VIP tier can be dramatic:

**Example: $100,000 round-trip trade**
- Kraken Regular: $420 in fees (0.42%)
- Kraken Expert: $180 in fees (0.18%)
- **Savings: $240 per round trip**

For active arbitrage traders doing multiple round trips daily, VIP tier access is essential for profitability.

## How to Reach Higher Tiers Faster

- **Focus volume on one exchange:** Spreading across many exchanges means lower tier on each
- **Trade high-volume pairs:** BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT count toward your volume
- **Use maker orders:** They count the same toward volume but cost less
- **Check for promotions:** Some exchanges offer temporary volume bonuses
