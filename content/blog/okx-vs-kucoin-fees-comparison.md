OKX and KuCoin are both popular alternatives to Binance, especially in regions where Binance faces regulatory restrictions. But how do their fee structures compare? Let us dive in.

## Trading Fee Comparison

| Feature | OKX | KuCoin |
|---------|-----|--------|
| Base maker fee | 0.08% | 0.10% |
| Base taker fee | 0.10% | 0.10% |
| Token discount | None | 20% with KCS |
| Effective taker | 0.10% | 0.08% (with KCS) |
| VIP maker floor | 0.02% | 0.025% |
| VIP taker floor | 0.05% | 0.05% |

**For makers:** OKX wins with 0.08% vs KuCoin's 0.10% (or 0.08% with KCS)

**For takers:** KuCoin with KCS discount (0.08%) beats OKX (0.10%)

## Withdrawal Fee Comparison

### BTC
- OKX: 0.0001 BTC (~$6.75)
- KuCoin: 0.0005 BTC (~$33.75)

### USDT (TRC-20)
- OKX: $0.80
- KuCoin: $1.00

### ETH
- OKX: 0.0004 ETH (~$1.40)
- KuCoin: 0.003 ETH (~$10.50)

### SOL
- OKX: 0.008 SOL (~$1.20)
- KuCoin: 0.01 SOL (~$1.50)

**Winner: OKX across the board for withdrawals.** KuCoin's BTC and ETH withdrawal fees are notably high.

## Liquidity Comparison

OKX generally has higher trading volume and tighter spreads than KuCoin. For major pairs like BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT, OKX is the more liquid exchange. However, KuCoin has a wider selection of altcoins, including many smaller tokens not listed on OKX.

## Unique Features

### OKX
- Excellent trading API
- Support for multiple blockchain networks
- Built-in Web3 wallet
- Strong derivatives market

### KuCoin
- Huge altcoin selection (700+ tokens)
- Trading bot marketplace
- Lending and earning features
- KCS dividend sharing

## For Arbitrage Traders

The best arbitrage corridor between these two is **OKX → KuCoin** due to:
- KuCoin's wider altcoin selection creates more pricing inefficiencies
- OKX's lower withdrawal fees make it cheaper to transfer out
- Different user bases lead to persistent price differences on mid-cap coins
