Crypto arbitrage is one of the oldest and most reliable trading strategies in the digital asset markets. By exploiting price discrepancies for the same asset across different exchanges, traders can lock in low-risk profits. In this guide, we take a deep dive into comparing spreads between **Binance** and **Kraken**, two of the largest global exchanges.

## Understanding Exchange Price Discrepancies
Why do prices differ between exchanges? The short answer is local market dynamics. Price discovery on Binance is driven by its massive volume, while Kraken has unique liquidity pools, fiat off-ramps, and local regional demands. 

During periods of high market volatility, these differences widen, creating profitable spreads.

## Step-by-Step Arbitrage Execution Process
To successfully execute a cross-exchange arbitrage trade, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Spread:** Use our [Arbitrage Pairs Tool](/arbitrage) to scan live price spreads.
2. **Buy on the Cheaper Exchange:** Acquire the asset (e.g., SOL) on the exchange with the lower ask price.
3. **Transfer the Asset:** Withdraw the asset from the buying exchange and deposit it onto the selling exchange.
4. **Sell on the Higher Exchange:** Market-sell the asset on the exchange with the higher bid price.

> **Crucial Warning:** Spreads can vanish within seconds. Always factor in transfer confirmation speeds before sending funds.

## Calculating True Net Profit
Never calculate your profit based on raw price differences alone! You must subtract the following fees:
* **Maker/Taker Trading Fees:** Incurred on both the buying and selling exchanges.
* **Withdrawal Gas Cost:** The flat fee paid to transfer the asset between exchanges.
