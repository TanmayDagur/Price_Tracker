Binance and OKX are both top-tier exchanges, but their fee structures differ in subtle ways that can significantly impact your trading profitability. Let us compare them head-to-head.

## Trading Fees at a Glance

| Feature | Binance | OKX |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Base maker fee | 0.10% | 0.08% |
| Base taker fee | 0.10% | 0.10% |
| Native token discount | 25% with BNB | None |
| Effective maker fee | 0.075% | 0.08% |
| Effective taker fee | 0.075% | 0.10% |

**For makers:** OKX has a lower base maker fee (0.08% vs 0.10%), but Binance wins when you factor in the BNB discount (0.075%).

**For takers:** Binance is cheaper at 0.075% (with BNB) vs OKX's 0.10%.

## Liquidity and Spread

Binance has the highest trading volume globally, which generally means tighter bid-ask spreads. OKX comes in second but still offers excellent liquidity on major pairs.

For arbitrage, the slight liquidity difference means Binance prices tend to be the "reference" price while OKX sometimes lags, creating spread opportunities.
