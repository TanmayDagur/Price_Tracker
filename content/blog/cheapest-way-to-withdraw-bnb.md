BNB (Build and Build, formerly Binance Coin) is the native token of the Binance ecosystem and the BNB Smart Chain. As one of the top 5 cryptocurrencies by market cap, BNB is widely traded. Here is how to minimize your withdrawal costs.

## BNB Withdrawal Fee Comparison

### BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)
- **Binance:** 0.0005 BNB (~$0.30)
- **KuCoin:** 0.01 BNB (~$6.00)
- **OKX:** 0.002 BNB (~$1.20)

### BNB Beacon Chain (BEP-2)
- **Binance:** 0.001 BNB (~$0.60)
- **KuCoin:** 0.01 BNB (~$6.00)

**Cheapest option: Binance via BEP-20** at approximately $0.30.

## Why Binance Dominates BNB Transfers

It is no surprise that Binance offers the cheapest BNB withdrawals — it is their native token. The BNB Smart Chain was created by Binance, so they have the lowest infrastructure costs.

## BEP-20 vs BEP-2: Which Network?

- **BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain):** The more widely supported network. Used for DeFi, tokens, and most exchange deposits.
- **BEP-2 (Beacon Chain):** The older network, being phased out on many platforms.

**Always use BEP-20** unless the receiving exchange specifically requires BEP-2.

## BNB for Fee Discounts

Remember that BNB has utility beyond trading:

- **25% fee discount** on Binance when used for trading fee payment
- Required for gas on BNB Smart Chain DeFi protocols
- Can be staked for additional yield

If you trade on Binance, always keep some BNB in your spot wallet for the fee discount.
