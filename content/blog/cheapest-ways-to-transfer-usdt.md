Tether (USDT) is the most widely used stablecoin in the crypto ecosystem. However, transferring USDT between exchanges can quickly become expensive if you choose the wrong blockchain network. In this article, we outline how to compare gas fees and pick the cheapest networks for USDT.

## The Problem with Ethereum (ERC-20) Transfers
By default, many exchanges suggest using the Ethereum blockchain (ERC-20) to send USDT. During network congestion, ERC-20 withdrawal fees can spikes up to **$5 to $10 per transaction**. This flat fee can completely wipe out smaller trading capitals and arbitrage spreads.

## Comparison of Popular USDT Network Options

Here is a breakdown of the typical networks supported by exchanges:

* **TRON (TRC-20):** Very fast (1-2 minutes) and typically costs around **$1 to $2** across most exchanges.
* **Solana (SOL):** Extremely fast (sub-minute) and typically costs **$1** or less.
* **BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20):** Often the cheapest option on Binance and Bybit, costing around **$0.50**.

## How to Verify Network Support
Before sending USDT across exchanges:
1. Double-check that **both** the sender and receiver exchanges support the selected network (e.g., TRC-20). Sending funds to an unsupported network will result in **permanent loss of assets**.
