Kraken and KuCoin serve different segments of the crypto market. Kraken is known for security and regulatory compliance, while KuCoin offers a massive altcoin selection and competitive fees. Here is how they compare.

## Trading Fee Comparison

| Feature | Kraken | KuCoin |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Base maker fee | 0.16% | 0.10% |
| Base taker fee | 0.26% | 0.10% |
| Token discount | None | 20% with KCS |
| Effective taker | 0.26% | 0.08% (with KCS) |

**Winner: KuCoin.** The difference is substantial — Kraken's base taker fee is 2.6x higher than KuCoin's.

## Withdrawal Fee Comparison

### BTC
- Kraken: 0.00015 BTC (~$10.12)
- KuCoin: 0.0005 BTC (~$33.75)

### ETH
- Kraken: 0.0015 ETH (~$5.25)
- KuCoin: 0.003 ETH (~$10.50)

### USDT (TRC-20)
- Kraken: $2.50
- KuCoin: $1.00

### SOL
- Kraken: 0.015 SOL (~$2.25)
- KuCoin: 0.01 SOL (~$1.50)

**Mixed results:** Kraken wins on BTC and ETH withdrawals, KuCoin wins on USDT and SOL.

## Security

- **Kraken:** No major hacks. Proof of reserves audited. Fully regulated in the US and EU.
- **KuCoin:** Experienced a $280M hack in 2020 (most funds recovered). Not regulated in many jurisdictions.

## Altcoin Selection

- **Kraken:** ~200 tokens
- **KuCoin:** ~700+ tokens

KuCoin wins by a landslide for altcoin diversity. If you trade smaller coins, KuCoin is the better choice.

## For Arbitrage Traders

The wide fee difference between Kraken and KuCoin creates interesting dynamics:

- Kraken's higher fees can lead to slightly different pricing
- KuCoin's altcoin depth creates unique arbitrage opportunities on smaller tokens
- The best corridor is buying on KuCoin (lower fees) and profiting from Kraken's price premium
