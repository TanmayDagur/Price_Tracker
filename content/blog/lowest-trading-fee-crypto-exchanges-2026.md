Trading fees are the silent killer of crypto profits. Whether you are an arbitrageur, day trader, or swing trader, the exchange you choose directly impacts your bottom line. Here are the top 5 exchanges with the lowest trading fees in 2026.

## 1. Binance

- **Maker fee:** 0.1% (0.075% with BNB discount)
- **Taker fee:** 0.1% (0.075% with BNB discount)
- **VIP discounts:** Up to 0.02% maker / 0.04% taker at highest tier
- **Unique advantage:** 25% fee discount when paying fees with BNB token

Binance remains the global leader in low fees. The BNB discount is a no-brainer — simply hold some BNB in your account and enable it in settings.

## 2. OKX

- **Maker fee:** 0.08%
- **Taker fee:** 0.1%
- **VIP discounts:** Down to 0.02% maker / 0.05% taker
- **Unique advantage:** Competitive maker fees even at base tier

OKX is arguably the best for makers, with a base maker fee of just 0.08% — lower than Binance without needing any token discount.

## 3. KuCoin

- **Maker fee:** 0.1%
- **Taker fee:** 0.1%
- **VIP discounts:** Down to 0.025% maker / 0.05% taker
- **Unique advantage:** KCS token holders get 20% fee discount

KuCoin matches Binance's base fees and offers a similar native token discount through KCS.

## 4. Bybit

- **Maker fee:** 0.1%
- **Taker fee:** 0.1%
- **VIP discounts:** Down to 0.02% maker / 0.04% taker
- **Unique advantage:** Strong derivatives market with competitive spot fees

Bybit has rapidly improved its spot trading fees and now competes directly with Binance and KuCoin.

## 5. Kraken

- **Maker fee:** 0.16%
- **Taker fee:** 0.26%
- **Pro discounts:** Down to 0.00% maker / 0.10% taker
- **Unique advantage:** Excellent fiat on-ramp and regulatory compliance

Kraken has the highest base fees on this list, but its professional tiers can bring costs down significantly for high-volume traders.

## Which Exchange Is Best for Arbitrage?

For arbitrage specifically, you need accounts on **multiple exchanges**. The ideal setup is:

1. **Binance** as your primary (lowest all-around fees)
2. **OKX** or **KuCoin** as your secondary
3. **Kraken** as a third for wider spread opportunities

The price differences between Kraken and Binance are often the widest because of Kraken's different user base and fee structure.
