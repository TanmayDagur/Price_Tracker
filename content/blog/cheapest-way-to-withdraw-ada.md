Cardano (ADA) is known for its low transaction fees on the native network, but exchange withdrawal costs can vary. Here is how to find the cheapest way to move your ADA.

## ADA Withdrawal Fee Comparison

- **Binance:** 1.0 ADA (~$0.45)
- **Kraken:** 0.6 ADA (~$0.27)
- **OKX:** 0.8 ADA (~$0.36)
- **KuCoin:** 2.0 ADA (~$0.90)
- **Coinbase:** Dynamic (~$0.10–$0.50)

**Cheapest options:** Coinbase and Kraken consistently offer the lowest ADA withdrawal fees.

## Cardano Network Advantages

The Cardano blockchain offers:

- **Low native fees:** On-chain transactions cost approximately 0.17 ADA (~$0.08)
- **Fast settlement:** Transactions confirm in about 20 seconds
- **Single network:** Unlike USDT which has multiple networks, ADA only operates on the Cardano network — no risk of choosing the wrong chain

## ADA as a Transfer Medium

Cardano can be used as a cheap transfer vehicle between exchanges:

1. Convert your asset to ADA (one trade fee)
2. Withdraw ADA (fee: $0.27–$0.90)
3. Deposit and convert back (one trade fee)

This is competitive with XRP and SOL for cross-exchange value transfer.

## ADA Arbitrage Potential

ADA-USDT shows moderate price discrepancies across exchanges:

- Typical spread: 0.05–0.25%
- Best corridors: Binance vs Kraken, OKX vs KuCoin
- Low withdrawal fees make even small spreads potentially profitable
