When it comes to moving money between crypto exchanges quickly and cheaply, XRP is hard to beat. With transaction fees under $0.01 and settlement times of 3–5 seconds, it has become the go-to transfer coin for savvy traders.

## XRP Transfer Costs by Exchange

- **Binance:** 0.25 XRP (~$0.15) withdrawal fee
- **Kraken:** 0.02 XRP (~$0.01) withdrawal fee
- **OKX:** 0.1 XRP (~$0.06) withdrawal fee
- **KuCoin:** 0.5 XRP (~$0.30) withdrawal fee
- **Coinbase:** Dynamic (~$0.01–$0.05)

The actual XRP Ledger network fee is a fraction of a cent. The rest is the exchange's markup.

## Why XRP Is Ideal for Transfers

### Speed
XRP Ledger settles transactions in **3–5 seconds**. Compare this to:
- Bitcoin: 10–60 minutes
- Ethereum: 1–15 minutes
- Solana: 1–30 seconds

### Cost
The XRP Ledger charges approximately $0.0002 per transaction. Even with exchange markups, total transfer cost stays under $0.50 in most cases.

### Universal Support
Every major exchange supports XRP deposits and withdrawals. Unlike Solana SPL tokens or BEP-20, you never have to worry about network compatibility.

## Using XRP as a Value Transfer Vehicle

Even if you are not trading XRP, you can use it to cheaply move value:

1. On Exchange A: Convert USDT → XRP (one trade fee)
2. Withdraw XRP to Exchange B (~$0.10)
3. On Exchange B: Convert XRP → USDT (one trade fee)

**Total cost:** Two trading fees + ~$0.10 withdrawal fee

Compare this to a direct USDT transfer on ERC-20: $3–$10.

## Price Risk Consideration

The downside of using XRP as a transfer medium is brief exposure to XRP price volatility. During the 3–10 seconds your funds are in transit, XRP's price could move.

For most practical purposes, a 5-second exposure window has negligible risk. Even a 1% price swing on a $10,000 transfer would only be $100, but a 1% move in 5 seconds is extremely rare.
