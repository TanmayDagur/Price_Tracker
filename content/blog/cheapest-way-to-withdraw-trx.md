Tron (TRX) is known for its extremely low transaction fees, and the TRC-20 network is one of the most popular for transferring stablecoins. Here is how TRX withdrawal fees compare across exchanges.

## TRX Withdrawal Fee Comparison

- **Binance:** 1.0 TRX (~$0.10)
- **Kraken:** 0.01 TRX (~$0.001)
- **OKX:** 1.0 TRX (~$0.10)
- **KuCoin:** 1.0 TRX (~$0.10)

**Cheapest option: Kraken** with an almost negligible fee of $0.001.

## Why Tron Is Perfect for Transfers

Tron was designed for fast, cheap transactions:

- **Near-zero fees:** On-chain TRX transfers cost fractions of a penny
- **3-second blocks:** Transactions confirm almost instantly
- **TRC-20 token standard:** USDT on TRC-20 is the most popular stablecoin transfer method globally
- **Wide support:** Every major exchange supports Tron

## TRC-20 vs Native TRX

When withdrawing from an exchange, you might see options for:

- **TRX (native):** Transferring the TRX token itself
- **TRC-20:** Transferring tokens built on Tron (like USDT-TRC20)

Both use the same Tron network and have similar fees. The key difference is what asset you are moving.

## Using Tron for Cross-Exchange Transfers

USDT on TRC-20 is the gold standard for cheap stablecoin transfers:

- No price risk (it is a stablecoin)
- $1.00 or less on most exchanges
- Confirms in seconds
- Available on virtually every exchange

This is why most arbitrage traders use USDT-TRC20 to rebalance funds between exchanges.
