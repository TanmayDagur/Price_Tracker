Not all trading pairs are created equal when it comes to arbitrage. Some pairs consistently show wider spreads between exchanges, while others are too tightly priced to generate any profit after fees. Here are the best pairs to watch in 2026.

## What Makes a Good Arbitrage Pair?

A profitable arbitrage pair has these characteristics:

- **Consistent price discrepancies** across at least 2–3 exchanges
- **Sufficient liquidity** on both sides to execute trades without heavy slippage
- **Low withdrawal fees** for the base asset
- **Fast transfer times** to minimize price risk during transit

## Top 5 Arbitrage Pairs for 2026

### 1. BTC-USDT
The king of crypto always has some spread, though it is usually tight (0.05–0.15%). Best for large position sizes ($10,000+) where even small percentage gains translate to meaningful dollar amounts.

- **Typical spread:** 0.05–0.20%
- **Best exchanges:** Binance vs Kraken, Binance vs Coinbase
- **Transfer tip:** Use Lightning Network for near-zero transfer fees

### 2. SOL-USDT
Solana has become one of the best arbitrage assets due to its extremely fast and cheap transfers. The Solana network confirms transactions in under 1 second with fees below $0.01.

- **Typical spread:** 0.10–0.40%
- **Best exchanges:** Binance vs KuCoin, OKX vs Kraken
- **Transfer tip:** Native SOL network is the cheapest — no alternatives needed

### 3. XRP-USDT
XRP transfers settle in 3–5 seconds with fees under $0.01. This makes it ideal for rapid arbitrage cycling. The pair also shows wider-than-average spreads due to regulatory uncertainty pricing.

- **Typical spread:** 0.08–0.35%
- **Best exchanges:** Binance vs Kraken, OKX vs KuCoin
- **Transfer tip:** XRP Ledger is the only option and it is very cheap

### 4. DOGE-USDT
Dogecoin might be a meme, but its price discrepancies across exchanges are no joke. Smaller exchanges often lag behind Binance pricing, creating windows of opportunity.

- **Typical spread:** 0.15–0.50%
- **Best exchanges:** Binance vs KuCoin, Binance vs Gate.io
- **Transfer tip:** DOGE network fees are typically under $0.50

### 5. ETH-USDT
Ethereum shows moderate spreads. The challenge is the high withdrawal fees on ERC-20. Smart arbitrageurs use exchanges that support ETH withdrawals on Arbitrum or Optimism L2s.

- **Typical spread:** 0.05–0.20%
- **Best exchanges:** Binance vs Coinbase, Kraken vs OKX
- **Transfer tip:** Use Arbitrum or Optimism L2 to save $3–$8 per transfer
